My QR

Show this code at any Citizens Fast counter or merchant to pay into this account.

Using your QR

  1. Pick an account to receive money into.
  2. Show the code to a teller or merchant at a Citizens Fast counter.
  3. Share or download it to send to someone who wants to pay you.

The code contains your account details only. It never contains your PIN, password or OTP.
